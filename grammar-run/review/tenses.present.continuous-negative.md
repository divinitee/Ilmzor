## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about something you are not doing right now.  
   must use: I'm not or I am not plus a verb ending in -ing · exactly one sentence · must include right now · must use I

2. Write one sentence about something another person is not doing at the moment.  
   must use: isn't or is not plus a verb ending in -ing · exactly one sentence · must include at the moment · must be about another person

3. Write one sentence about something two or more people are not doing today.  
   must use: aren't or are not plus a verb ending in -ing · exactly one sentence · must include today · must use a plural subject

4. Write one sentence about a phone, computer or machine that is not working right now.  
   must use: isn't working or is not working · exactly one sentence · must include right now · must name a device or machine

5. Write one sentence about something you usually do but are not doing this week.  
   must use: I'm not or I am not plus a verb ending in -ing · exactly one sentence · must include this week · must use I

6. Write one sentence telling a friend that they are not listening to you.  
   must use: aren't or are not plus listening · exactly one sentence · must use you as the subject · must use the verb listen

7. Write one sentence about someone in your family and something they are not doing today.  
   must use: isn't or is not plus a verb ending in -ing · exactly one sentence · must include today · must name a family member

8. Write two sentences: first say what someone is not doing now, then say what they are doing instead.  
   must use: a negative and a positive sentence in the present continuous · exactly two sentences · first sentence negative, second sentence positive · the same person in both sentences

## express (5)

1. Describe three things you are not doing right now, and say what you are doing instead. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present continuous negative with I'm not or I am not · look for: I'm not or I am not · verbs ending in -ing · right now or another now marker

2. A friend calls and asks you to meet. Explain why you can't: say what you are and aren't doing at the moment. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present continuous negative and positive for actions happening now · look for: isn't, aren't or I'm not · correct -ing spelling · at the moment or right now

3. Describe what the people in your home are not doing right now. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present continuous negative with isn't and aren't · look for: isn't with singular subjects · aren't with plural subjects · no don't or doesn't before -ing

4. Describe a day that is different from usual. What are you and your family not doing today? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present continuous negative for temporary situations today · look for: today as the time marker · am not, isn't or aren't plus -ing · a contrast with the usual routine

5. Look out of a window, or imagine a busy street. Describe what people are not doing at the moment. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present continuous negative with singular and plural subjects · look for: isn't with singular subjects · aren't with plural subjects · at the moment or right now
